# 🌹 Mujahida — Happy Birthday | Ishaq's Eternal Obsession

An intimate, toxic-romantic luxury birthday website crafted for **Mujahida** from **Ishaq**.

Designed with **Three.js 3D animations**, dynamic golden particle fields, ambient dark-romance soundscapes, a non-scrolling 3D media vault, and a wax-sealed interactive confession letter.

---

## ✨ Features

- **Obsidian & Champagne Gold Luxury Theme**: Seductive midnight obsidian, molten champagne gold, and poison crimson velvet undertones.
- **Three.js 3D Interactive Canvas**: Rotating 3D metallic torus knot, orbiting crystal shards, and drifting stardust embers reacting to mouse & gyro movement.
- **The Obsidian Vault (3D Spatial Deck)**: A unique non-scrolling 3D coverflow stage showcasing all photos and videos with cinema mode and audio controls.
- **"Ishaq's Confession" Letter**: Interactive 3D wax seal stamped with `I&M` that cracks open with a golden confetti burst to reveal an intimate, mature letter.
- **Eternal Flame & Champagne Toast**: Interactive hold-to-charge connection meter and birthday champagne confetti cannon.
- **Atmospheric Lo-Fi Synthesizer**: Web Audio API ambient soundtrack with golden equalizer bars.
- **Fully Responsive**: Flawless experience across mobile, tablet, and ultra-wide displays.
